## What the image shows

A newspaper front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026. The lead story is the first-ever trilateral summit of Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen, shown shaking hands beneath the US, Chinese and Russian flags. Around it, six captioned vignettes cover the rest of the day: Grand Theft Auto VI rolling out worldwide with players entering Vice City; NASA's Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth, nearly 50 years after launch; the Crimean Bridge hit again; Anthropic's record $81.3 billion IPO, the biggest of all time; Democrat Hakeem Jeffries nominated as a candidate to become the first Black Speaker of the US House; and a Van Gogh painting selling for a record $190 million after a dispute over its Nazi-era history. The image also carries the day in its masthead — "THURSDAY 19 NOVEMBER 2026" — so the date it sums up is unmistakable.

## How to run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes `out/entry.png`, an exact copy of the `entry.png` shipped in this repository. The program uses only the Node 22 standard library, needs no network, and both runs produce byte-identical output.
